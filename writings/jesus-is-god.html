Why am I writing this?

I’ve heard some objections to Jesus being divine from many different groups, and I wanted to address the verses used to object this notion. Ultimately, I want the Biblical perspective. No matter what side you sit on, if the Bible cannot contradict itself, then we have to reconcile each of our beliefs with every verse.

What Am I Not Saying?

I am not saying that Jesus is a separate God than the Father. I am saying that there is one God, Yahweh, who exists as three persons, Father, Son, and Holy Spirit. This is not a writing on the Trinity, but I bring up this point because it relates to the statement that Jesus is God. He is not another God, but the same one God, Yahweh.

Summary

From my research into these verses, though there are some challenging verses that the opposition brings, all verses, when brought into context, harmonize to Jesus being God, Yahweh. Taking the opposition’s lens, there are some irreconcilable verses used in support of this claim, while every verse that the opposition uses (again from my research) has been reconciled.
