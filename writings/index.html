All Writings

In-depth examinations of the topics that come up most in open-air evangelism — worked through carefully from Scripture, with nothing to hide.

This is a collection of my published apologetic writings, where I tackle numerous topics within Christianity. The focus of these comes from my evangelistic efforts, wanting to both be faithful to the gospel, and to engage in inter-faith dialogues.

1 Peter 3:14–16

“But even if you should suffer for righteousness’ sake, you will be blessed. Have no fear of them, nor be troubled, but in your hearts honor Christ the Lord as holy, always being prepared to make a defense to anyone who asks you for a reason for the hope that is in you; yet do it with gentleness and respect, keeping a clear conscience, so that those who speak maliciously against your good behavior in Christ may be ashamed of their slander.”

As I finish more writings, this list will be updated. These are all living documents and subject to change — the last updated date on each writing helps track such revisions. They are listed in order of completion, and the later ones are more refined than the earlier ones; please be patient as I work through each of them.
