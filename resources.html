Gospel Tracts

Tracts for every conversation

A good tract can keep the Gospel in someone's hands long after the conversation ends. These are the two I reach for most.

$1,000,000 Gospel Tract

I use this in the courtroom analogy — where someone else walks in and pays my fine for me. I encourage people to read it afterwards, while continuing the analogy. It pairs perfectly with the illustration.

5 Questions Gospel Tract

This tract mirrors the question style I love using when sharing the Gospel. It's great for when you hand someone a Bible to remind them of the Gospel, and for when the other person doesn't have much time to talk.

Evangelism Posters

Signs from the streets

These are the posters I take out to open-air evangelism. Download any of them, print them large, and take them with you.

Good Enough to Get Into Heaven?

Ephesians 2:8-9

One of my favorites. It contrasts depending even slightly on yourself with trusting fully in Jesus — a clean setup for the courtroom analogy.

Download

Salvation Receipt

Romans 6:23

My go-to and calling card in Oxford. Eye-catching, shows forgiveness for everything, and leads with the bad news before landing on the best news.

Download

Jesus Christ Wants to Redeem You

Mark 8:35

If you want to convict people while still offering hope, this one has been really effective. I often tie my own story into it, showing which of these I'm guilty of.

Download

Questions about Christianity

Jeremiah 29:13

A more inviting, apologetical opener — great for college crowds. Starter questions that anyone can begin with. Make sure you actually have an answer for each of these yourself though.

Download

Holy Spirit Seal & New Creation

2 Cor 5:17 · Eph 1:13-14 · Mark 1:15

Wordy, but a solid reference when you're already in a conversation. Good for grounding someone in what actually happens at the moment of salvation.
